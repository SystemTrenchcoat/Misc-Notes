
In simple terms, how much damage you can take before you incur lasting effects. There are three levels of harm: minor, moderate, and major. You have 2 of each slot. If you would take another in that slot with the rest of that level full, it increases in level (ex. you take 2 levels of minor harm, and then suffer a third. You would then take a level of moderate harm instead). If this were to happen with major harm, you would then take long term harm.


| Harm      | Example                  |
| --------- | ------------------------ |
| Long Term | A Lost Limit             |
| Major     | A Series of Broken Bones |
| Moderate  | A Sprain                 |
| Minor     | A Bruise                 |
