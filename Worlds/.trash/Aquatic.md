
Your body is made for life beneath the waves. ==Gain the Gills, Fins or Tentacles, and Echolocation leaves from the [[Adaptation.canvas]]==. Discuss with your GM how you go about going on land. Some options include: assuming a human form when your gills are dry, you must be completely submerged in water for 8 hours daily, or suffer the "Dried Out" moderate harm, or travelling around in a suit full of water.

***It is highly recommended that you discuss with your GM and party if you intend to only dwell in the water. While this is an option, it may not be the best for your campaign.
